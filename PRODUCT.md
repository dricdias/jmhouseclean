## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: a single responsive landing page using the bundled Sites web starter, because the brief prioritizes a polished marketing page and a private deployable preview.

## Users

Homeowners and property hosts in the United States who want a trustworthy, detail-oriented house cleaning service and need to see the quality of the work before contacting Jeane.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Jeane Matos House Clean, make the service feel credible and premium, show real completed work, and turn visitors into phone or email inquiries.

## Positioning

A personal, detail-led house cleaning service presented through real before/after-quality home photography and a warm, direct contact experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, local search, or social media, scan the page on a phone or desktop, switch between English and Portuguese, review service areas and completed work, then call or email to discuss a cleaning need.

- Primary contact phone: (475) 800-6931.

- The page must use the supplied reference image as visual direction and the supplied home photos as real work examples.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Jeane Matos House Clean. The tone should be warm, capable, polished, and trustworthy. The visual direction follows the supplied reference: clean editorial composition, fresh green accent, warm white surfaces, strong typography, rounded image corners, and confident calls to action.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real completed-work photos: the supplied `fe*.jpg`, `c9*.jpg`, and WhatsApp `.jpeg` files in the project root.

## Product Principles

- Show the quality before asking for the inquiry.
- Make contact immediate and human.
- Keep claims honest and grounded in the work shown.
- Let bilingual access feel native, not bolted on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, descriptive image alt text, reduced-motion support, and touch-friendly controls. Preserve readable content in both languages.
